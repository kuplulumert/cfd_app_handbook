🔧 SOLVER SCHEMES
SIMPLE Semi-Implicit Method for Pressure-Linked Equations - Most widely used pressure-velocity coupling - Industry Standard Solver
SIMPLEC SIMPLE-Consistent - Improved convergence over SIMPLE - Better for complex geometries Solver
PISO Pressure-Implicit with Splitting of Operators - Better for transient flows - Multiple corrector steps Solver
PIMPLE Merged PISO-SIMPLE algorithm - Combines advantages of both - Good for large time steps Solver
Coupled Fully coupled pressure-velocity solver - Fastest convergence - High memory requirements - Premium CFD codes Solver
📐 DISCRETIZATION SCHEMES
First Order Upwind Simple, stable, highly diffusive - Good for initial convergence - Low accuracy Discretization
Second Order Upwind Higher accuracy than first order - Good stability-accuracy balance - Most Common Choice Discretization
Central Difference Second order accurate, symmetric - Can cause oscillations - Requires fine grids Discretization
QUICK Quadratic Upstream Interpolation - Third order accuracy - Good for structured grids Discretization
MUSCL Monotonic Upstream-centered Scheme - TVD scheme - Prevents oscillations Discretization
TVD Total Variation Diminishing - Prevents spurious oscillations - High accuracy with stability Discretization
WENO Weighted Essentially Non-Oscillatory - Very high order accuracy - Advanced research applications Discretization
🟢 GRADIENT METHODS
Green-Gauss Cell Based Cell-centered gradient calculation - Most Common Default - Good for most applications Gradient
Green-Gauss Node Based Node-based gradient calculation - More accurate on skewed grids - Higher computational cost Gradient
Least Squares Cell Based Least squares gradient reconstruction - Good for unstructured grids - Robust for poor quality meshes Gradient
Least Squares Node Based Node-based least squares - Higher accuracy than cell-based - More expensive computation Gradient
