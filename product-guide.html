Detailed Product Recommendations

Specific CFD settings and approaches for each appliance type

❄️
Refrigerator
Time Dependency: Steady State
Turbulence Model: k-ε Standard
Solver Method: SIMPLE
Typical Re Range: 10³ - 10⁴

🌊 Flow Characteristics

  • Natural convection dominated
  • Low velocity internal flows
  • Temperature stratification
  • Buoyancy-driven circulation
🌀
Washing Machine
Time Dependency: Transient
Turbulence Model: k-ω SST
Solver Method: PIMPLE
Typical Re Range: 10⁴ - 10⁵

🌊 Flow Characteristics

  • Highly transient rotating flows
  • Multiphase water-air interaction
  • Complex drum geometry
  • Variable speed and direction
🍽️
Dishwasher
Time Dependency: Steady State
Turbulence Model: k-ε Standard
Solver Method: SIMPLE
Typical Re Range: 10³ - 10⁴

🌊 Flow Characteristics

  • Spray jet impingement
  • Complex rack geometries
  • Heat and mass transfer
  • Detergent mixing effects
🌪️
Vacuum Cleaner
Time Dependency: Steady State
Turbulence Model: k-ω SST
Solver Method: SIMPLEC
Typical Re Range: 10⁴ - 10⁶

🌊 Flow Characteristics

  • High velocity suction flows
  • Complex internal geometries
  • Cyclonic separation effects
  • Pressure gradients and recirculation
🔥
Oven
Time Dependency: Transient
Turbulence Model: k-ε Standard
Solver Method: PIMPLE
Typical Re Range: 10³ - 10⁴

🌊 Flow Characteristics

  • Strong buoyancy-driven flows
  • Heat transfer dominated
  • Time-dependent heating cycles
  • Natural convection patterns
💨
Cooker Hood
Time Dependency: Steady State
Turbulence Model: k-ω SST
Solver Method: SIMPLEC
Typical Re Range: 10⁴ - 10⁵

🌊 Flow Characteristics

  • High velocity extraction flows
  • Grease and particle capture
  • External aerodynamics
  • Kitchen environment interaction
🌬️
Air Conditioner
Time Dependency: Steady State
Turbulence Model: k-ε Standard
Solver Method: SIMPLE
Typical Re Range: 10⁴ - 10⁵

🌊 Flow Characteristics

  • Room air distribution
  • Heat exchanger flows
  • Thermal comfort analysis
  • Condensate management
💨
Hair Dryer
Time Dependency: Steady State
Turbulence Model: k-ω SST
Solver Method: SIMPLEC
Typical Re Range: 10⁴ - 10⁵

🌊 Flow Characteristics

  • High velocity heated jets
  • Compact nozzle design
  • Heat transfer optimization
  • Noise reduction considerations
📡
Microwave
Time Dependency: Steady State
Turbulence Model: Laminar
Solver Method: SIMPLE
Typical Re Range: 10² - 10³

🌊 Flow Characteristics

  • Low velocity natural convection
  • Electromagnetic heating effects
  • Moisture transport
  • Ventilation requirements
