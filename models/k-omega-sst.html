k-ω SST Model

Hybrid model combining k-ω near walls with k-ε in freestream - industry standard for most applications

🛠️ Practical Applications

🎯 Excellent For:

  • • External aerodynamics (aircraft, automotive)
  • • Turbomachinery (compressors, turbines)
  • • Heat exchangers and internal flows
  • • Adverse pressure gradient flows
  • • General-purpose CFD analysis
  • • Industrial design applications

⚠️ Limitations:

  • • Free shear flows (still limited)
  • • Transitional flows (needs transition model)
  • • Rotating flows (needs rotation corrections)
  • • Very low Reynolds numbers
  • • Rough wall effects (needs modifications)

🎯 CFD Guidance - k-ω SST Model

✅ When to Use k-ω SST (Industry Standard)

  • General-purpose CFD - Best overall performance for most applications
  • External aerodynamics - Aircraft, automotive, wind engineering
  • Turbomachinery - Compressors, turbines, fans, pumps
  • Heat exchangers - Internal flows with heat transfer
  • Adverse pressure gradients - Superior separation prediction
  • Mixed flow types - Wall-bounded + free shear regions
  • Industrial CFD - Standard choice in commercial codes
  • Design optimization - Good balance of accuracy and cost

⚠️ When to Consider Alternatives

  • Pure free shear flows - k-ε might be better for jets, mixing layers
  • Transitional flows - Need transition SST or γ-Reθ models
  • Rotating flows - Need rotation/curvature corrections
  • Very low Re flows - Consider laminar or low-Re models
  • Rough walls - Need roughness modifications
  • Computational efficiency critical - k-ε might be sufficient
  • Research accuracy - Consider Reynolds Stress Model

Setup Tips & Best Practices

  • Grid requirements: y+ < 1 for best results, can work with y+ < 300 with wall functions
  • Initialization: Same as k-ω: k = 1.5(UI)², ω = k^(1/2)/(0.3L)
  • Boundary conditions: Standard k-ω BCs, ω wall treatment automatic
  • Convergence: Generally more robust than standard k-ω
  • Blending: F₁ function automatically switches between formulations
  • Recommended choice: Default for most industrial applications
