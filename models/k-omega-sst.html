SST

k-ω SST Model

Hybrid RANS model - Industry standard for aerospace, automotive, and general-purpose CFD

✅
Use k-ω SST When (Industry Focus)
Situation Why It Works Advice
Aerospace applications (aircraft, turbines) Industry standard, combines best of k-ω and k-ε Default choice for external aerodynamics
Automotive flows (external aero, under-hood) Excellent separation prediction, robust Standard for automotive CFD analysis
General-purpose CFD (unknown flow physics) Most versatile, handles wide range of flows Best starting point when flow physics unclear
Mixed internal/external flows (turbomachinery) Switches between k-ω (walls) and k-ε (freestream) Excellent for compressors, turbines, fans
Flow separation critical (diffusers, airfoils) Superior separation prediction vs k-ε Use when separation accuracy is important
⚠️
Avoid k-ω SST When (Industry Focus)
Situation Why It Fails Industry Advice
Simple industrial flows (pipes, HVAC, basic equipment) Overkill, k-ε is faster and adequate Use k-ε for basic industrial applications
Large-scale industrial systems (power plants) Higher computational cost than k-ε k-ε more practical for large systems
Transitional flows (laminar-turbulent transition) Cannot predict natural transition Use Transition SST for transition flows
Very low Re flows (Re < 1,000) Assumes fully turbulent conditions Use laminar or low-Re models
Strongly rotating flows (mixers, cyclones) Lacks rotation/curvature corrections Use RSM or rotation-corrected SST
Free shear dominated flows (far-field wakes) Still inherits some k-ω free shear limitations Consider k-ε or RSM for pure free shear
🔑
Industry Takeaway

✅ Industry Standard

Use k-ω SST as default for aerospace, automotive, and general-purpose CFD - most versatile model.

⚡ Best Compromise

Combines k-ω near-wall accuracy with k-ε freestream robustness - best of both worlds.

🎯 When Uncertain

Default choice when flow physics are unknown - most likely to give reasonable results.

Key Equations
Blending Function
$$F_1 = \tanh\left[\min\left(\max\left(\frac{\sqrt{k}}{\beta^* \omega y}, \frac{500\nu}{y^2 \omega}\right), \frac{4\rho k}{\sigma_{\omega 2} D_{\omega}^+ y^2}\right)^4\right]$$

Switches between k-ω (near wall) and k-ε (freestream)

Turbulent Kinetic Energy (k)
$$\frac{\partial k}{\partial t} + U_j \frac{\partial k}{\partial x_j} = P_k - \beta^* k \omega + \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}\left[(\nu + \sigma_k \nu_t)\frac{\partial k}{\partial x_j}\right]$$
Specific Dissipation Rate (ω)
$$\frac{\partial \omega}{\partial t} + U_j \frac{\partial \omega}{\partial x_j} = \alpha \frac{\omega}{k} P_k - \beta \omega^2 + \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}\left[(\nu + \sigma_\omega \nu_t)\frac{\partial \omega}{\partial x_j}\right] + 2(1-F_1)\frac{\rho}{\sigma_{\omega 2} \omega}\frac{\partial k}{\partial x_j}\frac{\partial \omega}{\partial x_j}$$

Cross-diffusion term (last term) is the key SST improvement

Turbulent Viscosity
$$\nu_t = \frac{a_1 k}{\max(a_1 \omega, \Omega F_2)}$$

Strain rate limitation prevents overprediction in adverse pressure gradients

Engineering Analogy

🔄 Smart Hybrid System

k-ω SST is like a smart hybrid car that automatically switches engines:

  • Near walls: "Electric motor (k-ω) for precision and efficiency"
  • Highway/freestream: "Gas engine (k-ε) for power and robustness"
  • Automatic switching: "Blending function decides which engine to use"
  • Best of both: "Combines precision with robustness seamlessly"
