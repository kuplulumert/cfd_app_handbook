RSM

Reynolds Stress Model

Seven-equation RANS model - Most accurate RANS for complex 3D flows and research

✅
Use RSM When (Industry Focus)
Situation Why It Works Advice
Complex 3D flows (swirling, rotating, curved) Accounts for anisotropic turbulence, no Boussinesq assumption Best choice for complex industrial flows
Research applications (high accuracy needed) Most physically complete RANS model Use when computational cost is justified
Strongly rotating flows (turbines, mixers, cyclones) Captures rotation effects naturally Standard choice for rotating machinery
Curved flows (diffusers, bends, secondary flows) Handles streamline curvature effects Better than eddy viscosity models for curvature
Validation studies (CFD benchmarking) Highest fidelity RANS available Use as reference for other model validation
⚠️
Avoid RSM When (Industry Focus)
Situation Why It Fails Industry Advice
Engineering design iterations (fast turnaround needed) 3-5x slower than k-ε, convergence challenges Use k-ε or k-ω SST for design iterations
Simple flows (straight pipes, basic geometries) Overkill, simpler models give same results Use k-ε for simple industrial flows
Large industrial systems (power plants, large HVAC) Prohibitive computational cost Use k-ε for large-scale systems
Preliminary studies (conceptual design) Too expensive for early design phases Start with simpler models, upgrade if needed
Limited computational resources High memory and CPU requirements Use two-equation models for resource constraints
Convergence-critical applications More difficult to converge than simpler models Use k-ω SST for robust convergence

Reynolds Stress Transport
Reynolds Stress Transport Equation
$$\frac{\partial \overline{u_i u_j}}{\partial t} + U_k \frac{\partial \overline{u_i u_j}}{\partial x_k} = P_{ij} + D_{ij} - \varepsilon_{ij} + \Pi_{ij}$$

Transport equation for each Reynolds stress component

Production Term
$$P_{ij} = -\overline{u_i u_k} \frac{\partial U_j}{\partial x_k} - \overline{u_j u_k} \frac{\partial U_i}{\partial x_k}$$

Exact production term - no modeling required

Dissipation Rate Transport
$$\frac{\partial \varepsilon}{\partial t} + U_j \frac{\partial \varepsilon}{\partial x_j} = C_{1\varepsilon} \frac{\varepsilon}{k} P_k - C_{2\varepsilon} \frac{\varepsilon^2}{k} + \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}\left[\left(\nu + \frac{\nu_t}{\sigma_\varepsilon}\right) \frac{\partial \varepsilon}{\partial x_j}\right]$$

Dissipation equation similar to k-ε model

Engineering Analogy

🎼 Full Orchestra

RSM is like a full symphony orchestra:

  • Complete ensemble: "Every turbulence component (instrument) tracked individually"
  • Complex coordination: "All 6 stress components interact naturally"
  • Maximum fidelity: "Can play the most complex compositions (flows)"
  • Expensive production: "Requires large venue (computational resources)"
  • Overkill for simple music: "Don't need orchestra for simple songs (basic flows)"
