k-ε
Turbulence

k-ε Turbulence Model

Two-equation RANS model solving for turbulent kinetic energy and dissipation rate. Like modeling turbulence as a energy cascade from large to small eddies.

\frac{\partial k}{\partial t} + U_j \frac{\partial k}{\partial x_j} = P_k - \varepsilon + \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}\left[\frac{\mu_t}{\sigma_k}\frac{\partial k}{\partial x_j}\right]
Energy cascade analogy
k-ω
Turbulence

k-ω Turbulence Model

Two-equation model with specific dissipation rate. Better near-wall behavior than k-ε. Like having different turbulence time scales for different regions.

\frac{\partial \omega}{\partial t} + U_j \frac{\partial \omega}{\partial x_j} = \alpha \frac{\omega}{k} P_k - \beta \omega^2

Turbulence

SST k-ω Model

Shear Stress Transport model blending k-ε and k-ω advantages. Like having the best of both worlds for different flow regions.

F_1 = \tanh\left(\arg_1^4\right), \quad \arg_1 = \min\left[\max\left(\frac{\sqrt{k}}{0.09\omega y}, \frac{500\nu}{y^2\omega}\right), \frac{4\rho k}{CD_{k\omega}\sigma_{\omega 2}y^2}\right]

LES
Turbulence

Large Eddy Simulation

Resolves large turbulent structures, models small ones. Like watching the big whirlpools while estimating the small ripples.

\frac{\partial \bar{u}_i}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}(\bar{u}_i \bar{u}_j) = -\frac{1}{\rho}\frac{\partial \bar{p}}{\partial x_i} - \frac{\partial \tau_{ij}}{\partial x_j}

Wall Functions

Near-wall treatment bridging viscous sublayer to log-law region. Like using a universal translator for wall-adjacent cells.

u^+ = \frac{1}{\kappa}\ln(y^+) + B, \quad y^+ = \frac{u_\tau y}{\nu}
