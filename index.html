Model Selection Guide

Choose the right turbulence model for your application

Reynolds Number

Re < 2000: Laminar → Re > 10⁵: RANS → Re > 10⁶: Consider LES

Computational Cost

RANS (fastest) → DES → LES → DNS (most expensive)

Flow Complexity

Simple geometry: k-ε → Complex separation: SST → Massive separation: DES/LES
