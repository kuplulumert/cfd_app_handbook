SIMPLE

SIMPLE

Semi-Implicit Method for Pressure-Linked Equations

🎯 Practical Applications

🏭 Industrial CFD
Most widely used in commercial software (ANSYS Fluent, STAR-CCM+)
🌊 Steady State Flows
Heat exchangers, mixing tanks, pipe networks, HVAC systems
⚡ Fast Convergence
Engineering design iterations, parametric studies
💰 Cost-Effective
Lower computational cost than coupled solvers

⚙️ CFD Guidance

✅ When to Use SIMPLE:

  • Steady-state simulations - Primary application domain
  • Moderate Reynolds numbers - Re < 10⁶ typically
  • Incompressible flows - Density variations < 10%
  • Standard geometries - Well-conditioned meshes
  • Engineering accuracy - 1-5% error acceptable
  • Limited computational resources - Memory/time constraints

❌ When to Avoid SIMPLE:

  • Highly transient flows - Use PISO or PIMPLE instead
  • Strong pressure-velocity coupling - Consider Coupled solver
  • Compressible flows - Density-based solvers preferred
  • Poor mesh quality - High skewness, aspect ratios > 100
  • Research accuracy needs - < 0.1% error required
  • Complex multiphase - VOF with high density ratios

✅ Advantages

  • Robust and stable convergence
  • Low memory requirements
  • Well-established and validated
  • Good for most engineering applications
  • Excellent documentation and support

⚠️ Limitations

  • Slower convergence than coupled methods
  • Poor performance on transient flows
  • Sensitive to under-relaxation factors
  • May struggle with strong buoyancy
  • Limited accuracy for research applications

🔢 Algorithm Steps

Step 1: Momentum Predictor
$$\frac{\partial \rho \mathbf{u}}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\rho \mathbf{u} \mathbf{u}) = -\nabla p^* + \nabla \cdot \boldsymbol{\tau} + \rho \mathbf{g}$$

Solve momentum equations using guessed pressure field p*

Step 2: Pressure Correction Equation
$$\nabla \cdot \left( \frac{1}{a_p} \nabla p' \right) = \nabla \cdot \mathbf{u}^*$$

Solve for pressure correction p' to satisfy continuity

Step 3: Update Fields
$$p^{n+1} = p^* + \alpha_p p'$$
$$\mathbf{u}^{n+1} = \mathbf{u}^* - \frac{1}{a_p} \nabla p'$$

Update pressure and velocity fields with under-relaxation

Step 4: Check Convergence
$$\text{Residual} = \frac{\sum |\nabla \cdot \mathbf{u}|_{\text{cells}}}{\sum |\mathbf{u}|_{\text{cells}}} < \epsilon$$

Check mass conservation and repeat if not converged

📊 Variables & Parameters

𝐮* (m/s)
Predicted velocity field from momentum equations
p* (Pa)
Guessed pressure field (previous iteration)
p' (Pa)
Pressure correction to satisfy continuity
αₚ (-)
Pressure under-relaxation factor (0.3-0.7)
aₚ (kg/m³/s)
Central coefficient from momentum discretization
ε (-)
Convergence tolerance (typically 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻⁶)

🧠 Engineering Analogy

🏗️ Construction Site Coordination

SIMPLE is like coordinating a construction project where pressure and velocity are two teams that must work together:

  • Velocity Team (Step 1): "We'll move materials this fast" - but they guess the pressure resistance
  • Pressure Supervisor (Step 2): "Wait, that creates traffic jams!" - calculates pressure corrections
  • Site Manager (Step 3): "Let's compromise" - updates both teams' plans with under-relaxation
  • Quality Inspector (Step 4): "Is material flow balanced?" - checks mass conservation

The "Semi-Implicit" approach: Like updating work schedules gradually rather than completely reorganizing every day - more stable but takes more iterations to perfect coordination.

⚙️ Key Features

🔄 Segregated Approach
Solves momentum and pressure equations separately
🎯 Under-Relaxation
Prevents oscillations, ensures stability
📈 Predictor-Corrector
Predicts velocity, corrects pressure iteratively
💾 Memory Efficient
Lower memory usage than coupled methods
🛡️ Robust
Stable convergence on most mesh types
⏱️ Steady State Focus
Optimized for time-independent problems
