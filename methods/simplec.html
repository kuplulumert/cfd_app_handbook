⚙️ CFD Guidance

✅ When to Use SIMPLEC:

  • Improved convergence needed - Faster than SIMPLE
  • Complex internal flows - Better momentum-pressure coupling
  • Steady-state focus - Optimized for time-independent problems
  • Moderate to high Reynolds numbers - Re > 10⁴
  • Parametric studies - Multiple design iterations
  • Standard turbulence models - k-ε, k-ω, RSM compatibility

❌ When to Avoid SIMPLEC:

  • Highly transient flows - PISO/PIMPLE more suitable
  • Very low Reynolds numbers - Re < 100 (creeping flows)
  • Compressible flows - Density-based solvers preferred
  • Extreme mesh skewness - May cause convergence issues
  • Multiphase with large density ratios - ρ₁/ρ₂ > 1000
  • Research-level accuracy - Consider coupled solvers

✅ Advantages

  • Faster convergence than SIMPLE
  • Better momentum-pressure coupling
  • Higher under-relaxation factors possible
  • Same memory footprint as SIMPLE
  • Robust for complex geometries

⚠️ Limitations

  • Still segregated approach limitations
  • Not optimal for transient flows
  • May oscillate on poor quality meshes
  • Slightly more complex implementation
  • Limited benefit for simple geometries

🔢 Algorithm Steps

Step 1: Momentum Predictor (Same as SIMPLE)
$$\frac{\partial \rho \mathbf{u}}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\rho \mathbf{u} \mathbf{u}) = -\nabla p^* + \nabla \cdot \boldsymbol{\tau} + \rho \mathbf{g}$$

Solve momentum equations using guessed pressure field p*

Step 2: Enhanced Pressure Correction
$$\nabla \cdot \left( \frac{1}{a_p - \sum a_{nb}} \nabla p' \right) = \nabla \cdot \mathbf{u}^*$$

Key difference: Modified denominator includes neighbor coefficients

Step 3: Consistent Velocity Correction
$$\mathbf{u}^{n+1} = \mathbf{u}^* - \frac{1}{a_p - \sum a_{nb}} \nabla p'$$

Consistent with pressure correction equation

Step 4: Pressure Update
$$p^{n+1} = p^* + \alpha_p p'$$

Higher under-relaxation factors possible (αₚ = 0.3-0.7)

📊 Variables & Parameters

aₚ (kg/m³/s)
Central coefficient from momentum discretization
Σa_nb (kg/m³/s)
Sum of neighbor coefficients - key SIMPLEC modification
p' (Pa)
Pressure correction with enhanced coupling
αₚ (-)
Pressure under-relaxation (0.3-0.7, higher than SIMPLE)
αᵤ (-)
Momentum under-relaxation (0.8-0.9, higher than SIMPLE)
ε (-)
Convergence tolerance (typically 10⁻⁴ to 10⁻⁶)

🧠 Engineering Analogy

🎯 Improved Project Management

SIMPLEC is like upgrading from basic to advanced project management software:

  • SIMPLE approach: "Each team updates independently" - can lead to conflicts
  • SIMPLEC improvement: "Account for neighbor team dependencies" - better coordination
  • Enhanced coupling: Like having real-time communication between departments
  • Faster decisions: Less back-and-forth because teams understand interconnections

The "Consistent" improvement: Like ensuring all departments use the same updated information simultaneously, rather than working with outdated data from other teams.
